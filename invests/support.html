Support

NuaInvests is currently a public product preview. There is no financial account, portfolio, transaction or application to access or troubleshoot.

Current availability: this website provides an overview, privacy notice and support contact only. It does not provide financial services or advice.

Common questions

Can I open or connect an account?

No. NuaInvests has no account registration, bank connection, brokerage connection or portfolio import.

Can I buy, sell or transfer investments?

No. NuaInvests does not execute trades, handle payments, hold assets or move money.

Does this site recommend investments?

No. Nothing here is personalised advice, a recommendation, an offer or a solicitation. The product principles describe a possible future direction only.

Can support review my portfolio or documents?

No. Do not send statements, holdings, account numbers, tax records, identity documents or other sensitive financial information.

Financial safety

Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent NuaInvests while asking for money, credentials, remote access, wallet keys or verification codes. This preview does not take deposits or contact people with investment opportunities.

Contact

For a question about these public pages, email bapardi@gmail.com. Include the page address and a concise description of the issue. Do not include sensitive financial or identity information.
