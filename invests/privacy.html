Privacy notice

Effective 15 August 2026

This notice covers this static public website only. NuaInvests does not currently provide an account, portfolio, bank connection, financial profile, trading service or other connected product through these pages.

Information this site collects

These pages contain no sign-up form, account system, analytics script, advertising tracker or NuaInvests cookie. The site does not ask you to submit holdings, transactions, income, goals, risk preferences, bank details, tax information or identity documents.

Technical hosting data

The provider that hosts and delivers this website may process ordinary technical records such as IP address, browser type, requested page, time and security events. Those records are controlled by the hosting provider and may be retained for delivery, reliability and abuse prevention.

When you contact support

If you choose the email link on the support page, your email application and provider handle the message. We receive the address, content and attachments you decide to send and use them only to understand and respond to your enquiry. Do not send account numbers, statements, identity documents, credentials, tax records or details of your investments.

Sharing and retention

Support messages may be processed by the email and infrastructure providers needed to deliver and protect them. We do not sell information from support enquiries or use it for behavioural advertising. Messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain an appropriate support record or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can browse these pages without contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, correct or delete personal information contained in a support exchange. Contact us using the address below so the request can be assessed and handled under applicable law.

Children

This public website is written for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children through forms or accounts. NuaInvests does not provide investment services to minors. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has sent personal information by email.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated before the privacy scope changes materially or any interactive service is introduced. Privacy questions can be sent to bapardi@gmail.com.
