Privacy policy

Effective 20 August 2026

NuaTalks brings chats, calls, private Circles, posts and Moments into one app. It is not a public people directory, an open feed or an advertising service.

Information processed

The app may process your Nua Account identifier and profile, contacts you explicitly invite, conversation and Circle membership, delivery metadata, messages, voice notes, reactions, posts, comments, selected attachments, call signalling, reports, blocks and technical diagnostics.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate participants, deliver private conversations and selected-audience posts, provide notifications, operate calling, enforce safety controls and diagnose failures. NuaTalks does not sell personal information, use private content for behavioural advertising or make it public.

Microphone, camera and notifications

Microphone or camera access is requested only when you deliberately create a voice message or start or accept a call. Active capture is visibly indicated. Notifications may use generic text until the authenticated app retrieves private details.

Sharing, storage and deletion

Conversation and Circle content is shared only with its intended audience and providers needed for authenticated delivery, storage, abuse prevention and notifications. Account and deletion controls are provided through the connected Nua Account. NuaHome does not automatically receive NuaTalks private content.

Service integrity

NuaTalks only reports a message, upload, call or safety action as successful after the responsible service confirms it. Features remain unavailable when the required service or permission cannot be verified.

Children and safety

NuaTalks is not listed in Apple's Kids category. A parent or guardian must approve and supervise a minor's use in accordance with applicable law. The app is not an emergency service.

Security and contact

Authenticated sessions and native secure storage are used to protect account state. No system can be guaranteed perfect. For access, deletion or privacy questions, email bapardi@gmail.com.
