Privacy policy

Effective 15 August 2026

NuaTalks is private messaging for invited people. It is not a public directory, public feed or advertising service.

Information processed

The app may process your Nua Account identifier and profile, contacts you explicitly invite, group membership, message delivery metadata, messages, reactions, selected attachments, voice messages, call invitations, reports, blocks and technical diagnostics.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate participants, deliver private conversations, provide notifications, operate calling when available, enforce safety controls and diagnose failures. NuaTalks does not sell personal information or use conversation content for behavioural advertising.

Microphone, camera and notifications

Microphone or camera access is requested only when you deliberately create a voice message or start or accept a call. Active capture is visibly indicated. Notifications may use generic text until the authenticated app retrieves private details.

Sharing, storage and deletion

Conversation data is shared only with its intended participants and providers needed for authenticated delivery, storage, abuse prevention and notifications. Local private state is cleared when the app backgrounds where practical. Account and deletion controls are provided through the connected Nua Account.

Children and safety

NuaTalks is not listed in Apple's Kids category. A parent or guardian must approve and supervise a minor's use in accordance with applicable law. The app is not an emergency service.

Security and contact

Authenticated sessions and native secure storage are used to protect account state. No system can be guaranteed perfect. For access, deletion or privacy questions, email bapardi@gmail.com.
