How can we help?

NuaTalks provides private chats, voice notes, audio and video calls, Circles, posts and Moments between invited Nua Account users.

Sign in and invitations

Sign in with the Nua Account that received the invitation. Review every invitation before joining. NuaTalks does not automatically discover or add people from your address book.

Chats, Circles and Moments

Only share private material with people you trust. Review the named audience before publishing a post or Moment. NuaTalks keeps an action unavailable when delivery, upload or membership cannot be verified.

Voice and video calls

Microphone and camera access is requested only when you choose a voice or video feature. Check device permissions and your connection if a call remains unavailable.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm the phone has an internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the app to refresh your secure session.
  • Check iOS Settings for microphone, camera and notification permissions.
  • Sign out on a lost or shared device and contact support promptly.

Contact

Email bapardi@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a concise description. Do not send passwords, verification codes or private conversations.
