How can we help?

VestaTalks provides private conversations between invited Vesta Account users.

Sign in and invitations

Sign in with the Vesta Account that received the invitation. Review every invitation before joining. VestaTalks does not automatically discover or add people from your address book.

Messages and calls

Only send private material to people you trust. Microphone and camera access is requested when you choose voice or video features. Calls and media remain unavailable when their secure service is not connected.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm the phone has an internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the app to refresh your secure session.
  • Check iOS Settings for microphone, camera and notification permissions.
  • Sign out on a lost or shared device and contact support promptly.

Contact

Email bapardi@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a concise description. Do not send passwords, verification codes or private conversations.
