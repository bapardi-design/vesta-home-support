How can we help?

NuaConnects is a private, invitation-only space for finite circles of family and trusted people.

Sign in and invitations

Use the Nua Account linked to your invitation. An invitation does not silently add you to a circle: review the circle and accept it deliberately. If an invitation has expired, ask its sender to create another.

Posts and privacy

Check the named audience before publishing. You can leave a circle, block a person or report content from the relevant member or content controls. Do not post emergency or time-critical requests.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm the phone has an internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the app to refresh the authenticated session.
  • Check iOS Settings if you previously denied photo, camera or notification access.
  • Sign out on a shared device when you finish.

Contact

Email bapardi@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a short description. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or private-circle media.
