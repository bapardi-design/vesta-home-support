NuaConnectsPrivacy

Privacy policy

Effective 12 August 2026

NuaConnects is an invitation-only private-circle experience. It does not provide a public feed, public people search or advertising profile.

Information processed

The app may process your Nua Account identifier and profile, circle membership, invitations, posts, captions, reactions, comments, reports, blocks, notifications and media that you deliberately select. Technical diagnostics and device identifiers may be processed to secure the service and deliver notifications.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, show content to the exact private circle you choose, deliver invitations and notifications, enforce blocks, investigate reports and protect the service. NuaConnects does not sell personal information, run behavioural advertising or make private-circle content public.

Media and permissions

Photo or camera access is requested only when you choose to add media. The app does not silently record audio or video. A post composer must identify its audience before publishing.

Sharing and providers

Private content is shared only with the selected circle and service providers needed for authenticated delivery, storage, moderation and notifications. Providers receive only the data needed for their function. NuaHome, NuaTalks and other Nua products do not automatically receive your private-circle content.

Retention and control

You can leave circles, remove your content where available, block people and report abuse. Account and deletion controls are provided through the connected Nua Account. Records needed for safety, fraud prevention or legal obligations may be retained for a limited period.

Children

NuaConnects is not listed in Apple's Kids category. A parent or guardian must approve and supervise a minor's account and private-circle participation in accordance with applicable law.

Security and contact

Authenticated sessions and native secure storage are used to protect account state. No system can be guaranteed perfect. For access, deletion or privacy questions, email bapardi@gmail.com.
