# Nua brand system

## Decision

`Nua` is the master brand. Product names are closed compounds with one capital
product word: `NuaHome`, `NuaTalks`, `NuaConnects`, `NuaInforms`, and
`NuaCares`.

The master mark is a lowercase `n` shaped as an open threshold. Its outer form
never changes. A compact inner signal identifies the product without creating a
different logo family.

## Product signals

- `NuaHome`: an open door and three voice bars.
- `NuaTalks`: two conversation strokes and a reply point.
- `NuaConnects`: three linked people or places.
- `NuaInforms`: a source point and three editorial lines.
- `NuaCares`: a protected person and a calm check.

## Colour

- Graphite `#0B1216`: master background and primary type.
- Warm white `#F6F3EC`: master mark and light canvas.
- Sea green `#18C998`: home, access, continuity.
- Coral `#FF6B5E`: voice, attention, human response.
- Clear sky `#43B8E8`: communication and connection.
- Signal gold `#F5C451`: information and verified highlights.

Every product uses graphite and warm white. Product accents do not replace the
master silhouette and never become full-page theme colours.

## Wordmark

Use the exact CamelCase product name with no hyphen or space. `Nua` is semibold;
the product word is regular. Use the icon alone only when the product name is
already visible or the surface is an operating-system app icon.

## Compatibility

Public names and artwork change to Nua. Existing bundle IDs, package names,
deep-link schemes, API paths, database keys, and installed-device credentials
remain valid until a separately tested migration can preserve every update and
household link.
