Privacy notice

Effective 15 August 2026

This notice covers this static public website only. NuaInforms does not currently provide a public app, account, feed, personalisation service or connected product through these pages.

Information this site collects

These pages contain no sign-up form, account system, analytics script, advertising tracker or NuaInforms cookie. The site does not ask you to submit reading history, interests, contacts or precise location.

Technical hosting data

The provider that hosts and delivers this website may process ordinary technical records such as IP address, browser type, requested page, time and security events. Those records are controlled by the hosting provider and may be retained for delivery, reliability and abuse prevention.

When you contact support

If you choose the email link on the support page, your email application and provider handle the message. We receive the address, content and attachments you decide to send and use them only to understand and respond to your enquiry. Do not send passwords, private documents, confidential source material or sensitive personal information.

Sharing and retention

Support messages may be processed by the email and infrastructure providers needed to deliver and protect them. We do not sell information from support enquiries or use it for behavioural advertising. Messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain an appropriate support record or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can browse these pages without contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, correct or delete personal information contained in a support exchange. Contact us using the address below so the request can be assessed and handled under applicable law.

Children

This public website is written for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children through forms or accounts. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has sent personal information by email.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated before the privacy scope changes materially or any interactive service is introduced. Privacy questions can be sent to bapardi@gmail.com.
