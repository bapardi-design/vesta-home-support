Support

NuaInforms is currently a public product preview. There is no app to install, account to recover or live information service to troubleshoot.

Current availability: this website provides an overview, privacy notice and support contact only.

Common questions

Can I create a NuaInforms account?

No. Account registration and sign-in are not available through this website.

Can I add a news source or receive alerts?

No. The site does not connect sources, ingest feeds, monitor topics or deliver notifications.

Does NuaInforms verify information?

Not through this preview. The product principles describe a future direction, not an active verification service. Check important claims against original sources.

Can I submit private or embargoed material?

No secure submission channel is provided. Do not send confidential source material, unpublished documents or sensitive personal information to the support address.

Contact

For a question about these public pages, email bapardi@gmail.com. Include the page address and a concise description of the issue. Do not send passwords, identity documents or confidential materials.
