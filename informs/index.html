A direction for better understanding

Information is more useful when its source, timing and uncertainty stay visible.

NuaInforms is being shaped as a calm way to examine important information, compare context and keep track of what changes. This site introduces that intent; it is not a news service or working product.

Available today

These public overview, privacy and support pages only. There is no account access, live feed, source ingestion, alert delivery or downloadable application.

Product principles

The standard future work would have to meet

Keep the source attached

Origins, publication time and material updates should remain easy to inspect instead of disappearing behind a summary.

Separate fact from interpretation

Claims, commentary and uncertainty should be presented distinctly so people can reach their own informed view.

Make correction visible

Changed or disputed information should retain useful history and context rather than quietly replacing the earlier record.

Clear boundary

No claim here is a promise of live coverage, completeness or verification.

NuaInforms does not currently publish a feed, monitor events, personalise information or send alerts. Important decisions should be checked against original sources and appropriately qualified professionals.
